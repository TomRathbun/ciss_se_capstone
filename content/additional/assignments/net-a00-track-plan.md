# NET-A00 — Networking Track Plan & Fabric Map

**Weight:** 5% · **Due:** After net-00-welcome · **Module:** net-00-welcome

## Prompt

Show you understand the **Juniper intern path** and the **Site-A / Site-B lab fabric** before you touch a commit.

## Deliverables

1. **Track plan** (½ page): module order you will follow; one access risk (console, vSRX, missing PE) and a mitigation.
2. **Fabric map:** table of devices (`ex-a-acc-01`, `ex-a-core-01`, `srx-a-fw-01`, `srx-b-fw-01`, `mx-pe-01`/`02`, optional encryptors) with **role** and whether your bench has hardware, vSRX/vMX, or **worksheet-only**.
3. **Addressing sheet:** copy the welcome prefixes, then note any **instructor remaps** (real IPs from the lab sheet).
4. **Three SE links:** one sentence each for interface/ICD, availability NFR, and verification evidence in network work.
5. **Integrity / safety rules** you will follow (≥ 5 bullets: commit confirmed, no PSK in Git, no scanning, encryptor photos, shared-box etiquette).

## Quality bar

- BGP is named **BGP**, not “BGB.”  
- Branch SRX is not claimed as a full MPLS PE.  
- Safety rules show restraint on shared older boxes.

## Rubric

| Dimension | Max | What we look for |
|-----------|-----|------------------|
| completeness | 10 | Plan + fabric + addressing + SE + safety |
| accuracy | 10 | Correct roles/platforms; honest lab vs worksheet |
| communication | 5 | Peer could use the map on Monday |
