# ADMIN-A10 — Ticket, Runbook & Resolution Pack

**Weight:** 5% · **Due:** After admin-10-documentation-tickets · **Module:** admin-10-documentation-tickets

## Prompt

Write operational documentation that **night shift could run** without calling you.

## Deliverables

1. **One incident ticket** (template filled): summary, impact, urgency, environment, steps to reproduce, evidence links, sanitization done.
2. **One service request** example (access/install) — show you know it is *not* an incident.
3. **One change note** (planned): risk, window, backout.
4. **Runbook (1 page):** for a recurring task (service restart check, cert expiry check, disk alert, etc.) with numbered steps and expected outputs.
5. **Resolution note** for the incident: root cause (or best hypothesis), fix, follow-ups.

## Quality bar

- Work types are not mixed.
- Runbook is executable by a peer.
- Secrets and personal data are scrubbed.

## Rubric

| Dimension | Max | What we look for |
|-----------|-----|------------------|
| ticket_quality | 10 | Actionable incident + correct types |
| runbook | 10 | Executable steps |
| communication | 5 | Clear resolution/follow-ups |
