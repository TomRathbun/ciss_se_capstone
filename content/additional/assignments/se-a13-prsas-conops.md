# SE-A13 — PRSAS CONOPS

**Phase:** capstone · **Weight:** 25% of capstone-SE · **Due:** Week 15 Thursday · **Module:** se-13

## Prompt

Write a **concise CONOPS** for the lab air picture. Expand SE-A10; do not replace it with a design spec.

## Deliverables

1. **Context** (½–1 page): where the watch sits, what is simulated.
2. **Stakeholders ≥ 6** and **4 needs** in course grammar, each `derives_from` the vision.
3. **Happy-path mission thread** (8–12 steps).
4. **Three unwanted threads** including dual-feed conflict and coast/drop.
5. **In ≥ 5 / out ≥ 5 / assumptions ≥ 3**.
6. **Logical data-flow sequence** (Mermaid) — no class names required.
7. Pointer to your **SE-A10** pack (link or attachment name).

## Quality bar

- Human-in-the-loop is explicit.
- No live AD, no auto-engage, no classified sites.
- A SW intern can list components after reading it.

## Rubric

| Dimension | Max | What we look for |
|-----------|-----|------------------|
| operational_clarity | 15 | Thread + unwanted paths are usable |
| scope_discipline | 10 | In/out honest; no gold plate |
| communication | 5 | 4–6 pages, not a novella |
