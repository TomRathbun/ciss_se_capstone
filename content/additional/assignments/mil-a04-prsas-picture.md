# MIL-A04 — Air Picture Annex & Watch Script

**Phase:** capstone · **Weight:** 100% of capstone-MIL · **Due:** After ops-04 · **Module:** ops-04-prsas-picture

## Prompt

Keep the fused picture **operationally honest** for the integration demo.

## Deliverables

1. One-page **picture annex**: what the operator sees, what it is not.
2. Glossary of 8 terms in *your* words (track, plot, Mode 3/A, coast, drop, conflict, source, stale).
3. **10-minute watch script** with operator and supervisor lines.
4. **Over-claim audit** — three sentences you refused to write, and why.
5. Demo roles: who speaks when.

## Quality bar

- No weapons employment, no real unit tracks, no IFF crypto.
- CONFLICT is a sensor disagreement, not a firing solution.
- A supervisor intern can read the annex aloud.

## Rubric

| Dimension | Max | What we look for |
|-----------|-----|------------------|
| operational_honesty | 15 | Picture vs C2 over-claim is clear |
| script | 10 | Runnable in the demo |
| communication | 5 | Annex fits one page |
