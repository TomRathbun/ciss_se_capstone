## Intended problem (draft)

Interns will specify (and later may prototype) a **radar situational awareness** capability that:

- Ingests **multiple tracks/feeds** (e.g. local radar + a second source)  
- Presents a **situation picture** to an operator  
- Raises **alerts** when rules fire (zone entry, lost track, dual-feed conflict)  
- Supports at least two roles (operator / supervisor)  

Exact feed definitions, scoring, and deliverables will be published when this phase opens.

## How to prepare now

1. Finish A1–A5 with strong testability.  
2. Practice RTMs.  
3. Keep ops vocabulary from ATO modules.  
4. Watch the ETAS case study for interface discipline — feeds are interfaces.
