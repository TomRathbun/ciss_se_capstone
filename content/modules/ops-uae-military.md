### Ground-based air defence — including **SHORAD** (public)

**SHORAD** = **Sho**rt-**R**ange **A**ir **D**efence: point / low-altitude defence against aircraft, helicopters, cruise missiles, rockets, and **UAVs**. It sits **below** long-range SAMs and ballistic-missile defence in a **layered** architecture.

Open sources describe UAE ground-based air defence as a **multi-layer stack** (US upper/mid tiers + Russian SHORAD + other partners + domestic C-UAS themes). Use this for **CONOPS language and interfaces** — not basing, battery counts as secrets, or employment tactics.

![Layered air defence and SHORAD concept diagram (training)](/static/images/uae-military/systems/layered-ad-shorad.svg)

*Classroom concept only — not a real deployment map.*

#### Layer map (open-source vocabulary)

| Layer (concept) | Example families in **open reporting** | Origin (typical) | What interns should remember |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|------------------|------------------------------|
| **Upper-tier BMD** | **THAAD** | United States | High-altitude / ballistic intercept theme; UAE reported as early export customer |
| **Long / medium SAM** | **MIM-104 Patriot** (PAC-2 / PAC-3 themes) | United States | Mid-to-long atmospheric layer; major infrastructure defence theme in open press |
| **Medium SAM (partner)** | **KM-SAM / Cheongung-class** themes; **Barak**-class themes | ROK; Israel (open press) | Multi-origin mid-tier options appear in open defence press — **verify names; no performance claims** |
| **SHORAD / point defence** | **Pantsir-S1** (SA-22 family) | Russia | **Primary SHORAD vocabulary for UAE in open sources** — launch customer; missile **+** gun combination for low-altitude / drone / aircraft threats |
| **Legacy medium/low** | **MIM-23 I-HAWK** (Phase II/III themes) | United States | Older medium/low-altitude layer still cited in open Gulf AD surveys |
| **VSHORAD / MANPADS** | **Mistral** (ATLAS-type mounts), **RBS 70** themes | France / Sweden | Very short range; man-portable or light mounts in open historical lists |

### Radar systems (open-source vocabulary)

Radars are the **sensors** that make every AD and SA story real. For CISS, treat each radar family as a possible **external system** on a context diagram and as an **ICD party** (track messages, quality, rate, ID confidence).

![Radar roles concept diagram (training)](/static/images/uae-military/systems/radar-roles.svg)

*Classroom concept only — not a site map or classified sensor architecture.*

#### How to group radars (teaching model)

| Role | What it does | Example families in **open reporting** |
|------|--------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Volume / early-warning search** | Wide air picture for COP and cueing | Thales **Ground Master GM400 / GM403** family (long-range 3D AESA themes; UAE GM-403 appears in open media) |
| **Missile-defence fire-control** | Detect, track, discriminate ballistic threats for interceptors | **AN/TPY-2** (THAAD sensor); Patriot **engagement / fire-control** radars |
| **Medium SAM fire-control** | Engagement-quality tracks for mid-tier SAMs | Radars organic to **Patriot**, **I-HAWK** (legacy), **KM-SAM / Cheongung-class** themes |
| **SHORAD / tactical multi-mission** | Low altitude, UAV, C-RAM sense-and-warn | **Pantsir** organic radar/EO; Saab **Giraffe** family themes (land/naval) |
| **Naval air/surface search** | Shipboard air picture and self-defence cueing | **Sea Giraffe** family (open lists associate variants with UAE Navy) |
| **Airborne fighter radar** | Organic fire-control / SA for fighters | AESA/fire-control radars on **F-16 Block 60**, **Mirage 2000-9**, **Rafale** (platform-level, not national GBAD) |
| **Civil / dual-use ATC** (context only) | En-route / approach pictures | Not military inventory — still appear as **external feeds** in some SA designs |

#### Radar integrity rules

- **No basing maps.** Do not guess which hill or base has which radar.  
- **No classified ranges.** Manufacturer *marketing* numbers are not your FR baseline unless the customer publishes them.  
- **Feeds are interfaces.** A radar outage is an **IF/THEN** and ICD problem (degraded COP, cue loss).  
- **Mode matters.** Search vs track vs fire-control vs terminal BMD modes change data rates and consumers.

#### SE habits for radar

| Habit | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Context diagram | Actor “Radar GM403” → SA system → COP display |
| ICD fields | Track ID, lat/lon/alt, velocity, quality, time, classification confidence |
| NFR | Update rate, availability, encryption of track feed |
| EARS FR | WHEN a valid track is received from Sensor X, the SA system shall … |
| Validation | Ops SME walkthrough: “does this picture match doctrine / COP needs?” |

## OV-1 operational overview (tie to Systems Engineering)

An **OV-1** (high-level operational concept graphic) shows *who fights with what, who senses, and who talks* — before you write shall-statements. It is not a wiring diagram and not a basing map.

Use this training OV-1 as a **shared picture** for:

- **Vision / CONOPS** language  
- **Context diagram** actors and external systems  
- **Interfaces** (track feeds, Link 16, VCS)  
- **Allocation** of later FRs to sensors, effectors, and C2  

![OV-1 style operational concept — layered AD, radars, weapons, C2 and communications (training)](/static/images/uae-military/systems/ov1-uae-ad-sa.svg)

*Unclassified open-source vocabulary only. Bubbles = sensors/platforms; boxes = weapon layers; dashed lines = voice/data/track flows.*

### How to read the OV-1

| Zone | What you see | SE habit |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **Air / space** | Combat air, support aviation, UAS, threat concept | Stakeholders + use cases (CAP, escort, ISR, swarm defence) |
| **Sensors · radars** | GM403, AN/TPY-2, Patriot FC, Giraffe, Pantsir EO/radar, Sea Giraffe | Each bubble is a candidate **ICD** party and COP feed |
| **C2 · COP · comms** | AOC hub, VCS voice, Link 16 / data nets | Sequence diagrams & IF/THEN for failover |
| **Effectors · layers** | THAAD → Patriot → medium SAM → Pantsir SHORAD → VSHORAD/C-UAS (+ land context) | Requirements **allocated_to** design elements by layer |

### From OV-1 → SE cascade (worked mini-example)

```text
VISION   Shared, defensible air picture and layered defence under time pressure
   │ derives_from
NEED     As AOC operators, we need fused tracks from volume-search and BMD sensors
         so that TEWA can prioritize threats before SHORAD is saturated
   │ traces_to
UC       Correlate dual-layer tracks; Fail over VCS when Link 16 degrades
   │ allocated_to
FR       WHEN a valid GM403 track is received, the SA system shall …
         IF Link 16 is unavailable, THEN the C2 system shall …
   │ allocated_to
DESIGN   Ingest service · COP display · ICD to radar feed · VCS procedure link
```

### Workshop add-on (10 min) — walk the OV-1

1. Circle **three radars** and name one consumer each (COP, THAAD, Pantsir, …).  
2. Draw one **extra** interface you would add to the picture (e.g. weather, higher HQ).  
3. Write **one EARS FR** that only makes sense because two bubbles must talk.

## Self-check (5–8 min)

1. Name three **branches** of the UAE Armed Forces.  
2. Who is Commander-in-Chief **by role**, and where do you get an **official photo**?  
3. Does a **Captain (Naqib)** wear three **stars** or three **bars** in the teaching model?  
4. What does **SHORAD** stand for, and name one SHORAD family strongly linked to UAE in open sources.  
5. Order these layers high→low: Pantsir, THAAD, Patriot.  
6. Which radar family is the open-source **THAAD sensor**, and which family is a long-range **volume-search** example?  
7. True/False: You may invent a classified loadout for A5 if it “sounds realistic.”  

### Answers

1. Any three of: Army, Navy, Air Force, Presidential Guard, Joint Aviation Command, National Guard.  
2. **President of the UAE**; official portrait from **u.ae / WAM / cabinet** — not a random social post.

4. **Short-Range Air Defence**; **Pantsir-S1** is the main open-source UAE SHORAD example (also discuss Mistral/RBS 70 / SkyKnight themes as VSHORAD/C-UAS).  
5. **THAAD** (upper BMD) → **Patriot** (long/medium SAM) → **Pantsir** (SHORAD / point defence).  
6. **AN/TPY-2** (THAAD); **Ground Master GM400/403** (volume search) — or equivalent from the catalogue.
