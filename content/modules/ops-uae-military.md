## Structure of the UAE Armed Forces (public)

The **United Arab Emirates Armed Forces** are a federal force. Publicly described components include:

| Component | Role (plain language, public) |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| **UAE Army** | Land forces |
| **UAE Navy** | Maritime forces |
| **UAE Air Force (UAEAF)** | Air power — fighters, support aviation (public inventories) |
| **Presidential Guard** | Elite / special-mission force (publicly acknowledged branch) |
| **Joint Aviation Command** | Joint aviation (public branch listing) |
| **National Guard** | National Guard (public branch listing) |

GHQ / joint command sits under national defence leadership (see next section). Exact unit names and locations are **out of scope** unless provided officially in class.

**Industry note (public):** UAE invests heavily in defence industry clusters such as **EDGE Group** and related entities (e.g. public names like NIMR, ADSB, Caracal appear in open sources). Treat industry as **supplier / partner stakeholders** in SE terms — not as a source of classified performance data.

### Defence & industry partners (public context)

## Open-source weapon systems & platforms (public talking points)

Use these for **mission-card vocabulary** and stakeholder conversation — **not** for performance claims, basing, or real loadouts.

### Air (UAEAF — public)

| Family | Public notes | Planning / SE conversation |
|--------|--------------|----------------------------|
| **F-16E/F Block 60 “Desert Falcon”** | U.S.-origin multirole; widely reported backbone of UAEAF fighter force | Multirole / CAP / strike *concepts*; sensor + weapons *families* only |
| **Mirage 2000-9** | French-origin multirole; long public association with UAEAF | Mixed fleet → training, sustainment, and interface diversity |
| **Rafale** | Public reports of large French order (on order / modernization path) | Future fleet transition = long-horizon CONOPS & ICD change |
| **Support / rotary / training types** | Various public types in open inventories | Tanker/lift/rotary as **enablers** on mission cards |

**F-35:** Publicly discussed in past U.S.–UAE sale negotiations; treat as **policy/open reporting**, not as “in inventory unless official sources say so.”

### Air & missile defence / C2 (public themes)

| Theme | Public examples (families) | Intern takeaway |
|-------|----------------------------|-----------------|
| Layered air & missile defence | Public reporting on systems such as **Patriot**, **THAAD**, and other layered AD | Different layers → different **COP / C2 / TEWA** pictures |
| Sensors & warning | Public discussion of radars, early warning, space-enabled warning (global systems) | Feeds and interfaces for SA systems |
| Battle management | Public C2 modernization themes | Software + ops CONOPS co-evolve |

### Land & sea (very high level)

| Domain | Public examples | Use in class |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| Armour / vehicles | Publicly reported types (e.g. Leclerc among open reporting); local industry (NIMR, etc.) | Land support to joint vignettes — not deep tactics |
| Naval | Public corvette / patrol / shipbuilding (e.g. ADSB-related open reporting) | Maritime SA when mission set includes sea |
| Precision munitions | Public families (e.g. air-to-air and air-to-ground types associated with fighter sales in open press) | “Stores concept” on A5 — **families only** |

### UAS / emerging

Open sources discuss UAE interest in **UAVs / UCAVs** and advanced munitions via international and EDGE-related programmes. For class: “UAS as sensor or effector **stakeholder systems** on a context diagram” — no employment details.

## Self-check (5–8 min)

Answer before peeking.

1. Name three **branches** of the UAE Armed Forces (public list).  
2. Who is the **Commander-in-Chief** of the UAE Armed Forces by **role** (title), and how do you verify the person?  
3. Give the approximate English equivalent of **Naqib** and **Aqid**.  
4. Name two **publicly reported** UAEAF fighter families.  
5. Why does a mixed fighter fleet (e.g. U.S. + French types) matter for **systems engineering**?  
6. True/False: For Assignment A5 you may invent a classified weapons loadout if it “sounds realistic.”  

### Answers

1. Any three of: Army, Navy, Air Force, Presidential Guard, Joint Aviation Command, National Guard.  
2. **President of the UAE** is Commander-in-Chief; verify the incumbent on official UAE government sites before briefing a name.  
3. **Naqib** ≈ Captain; **Aqid** ≈ Colonel.  
4. Common open-source answers: **F-16E/F Block 60**, **Mirage 2000-9** (Rafale as public order/future path also acceptable if phrased carefully).  
5. Different vendors → different **interfaces, training, sustainment, and ICD** problems; CONOPS and requirements must not assume a single homogeneous system.  
6. **False** — unclassified / public concepts only; inventing classified detail is an integrity fail.
