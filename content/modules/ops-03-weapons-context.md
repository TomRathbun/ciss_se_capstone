# Weapons & Platforms Context (Lockheed Heritage)

> Training context only. **Public, unclassified** product knowledge.  
> Do **not** research or invent classified employment, loadouts, or tactics.

## Learning outcomes

| # | After this module you can… |
|---|----------------------------|
| 1 | Name major **platform and weapon families** in public vocabulary |
| 2 | Link platforms to **planning factors** (role, fuel, sensors, C2) |
| 3 | Write **questions for SMEs** instead of inventing employment details |

> **Facilitator tip:** ~10 min integrity rules, ~15 min table walkthrough, ~30 min one-page brief exercise. Kill any “secret sauce” speculation — reward good questions.

## Purpose

Give interns a **shared public vocabulary** for platforms and systems that appear in mission discussions — same spirit as the SDC Time Tracker **Tactical Library** / innovations gallery.

## How to use this module

1. Open the time tracker **Tactical Library** / innovations page when available (instructor URL; often on the case-study host).  
2. For each item below, say: *mission roles it supports*, not classified employment.  
3. Prefer **public** sources when you cite: manufacturer product pages, official public fact sheets, museums/history.  
4. Tie back to planning factors: range class, sensors, weapons **concept**, C2.  

## Representative categories

| Category | Example systems (public) | Planning conversation (ATO-relevant) |
|----------|--------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| 5th-gen fighter | F-35 Lightning II | Multi-role package option; sensor-rich SA concept; survivability *as public talking point only* |
| Air dominance | F-22 Raptor | Air superiority / escort concept in exercise language |
| Multirole fighter | F-16 Fighting Falcon | Common workhorse for CAP, training, and multi-role exercise packages |
| Airlift | C-130J, C-5 | Lift / logistics / special-mission variants — support the force, not “shooters” |
| Maritime patrol | P-3 Orion heritage | Maritime SA / patrol concepts when the mission set includes sea |
| Rotary | Black Hawk, CH-53K | Troop / heavy lift — usually support or joint vignette, not classic ATO fighter CAP |
| Munitions / strike | JASSM, LRASM, Hellfire, Javelin | Standoff vs direct-attack **families** at high level; never invent real target data |
| Missile defense | PAC-3, THAAD, Aegis | Layered defense → different C2 and COP pictures than pure air CAP |
| Space / C2 | GPS III, SBIRS, C2BMC | Timing, warning, battle management — **enablers** for every ATO (time sync, warning, C2 glue) |

## Exercise (30 min) — one-page brief

Pick **one air mission type** from the ATO planning module. Choose **two** platforms (primary + support). Deliver a **one-page brief** (markdown fine):

**Title:** Platform choices for \<mission type\>  
**Table:** Platform | Role (primary/support) | Public source you used (link or “Tactical Library”)  
**Bullets:**

1. Why primary fits the objective  
2. What support platform provides  
3. One fuel/tanker concern  
4. One sensors/SA concern  
5. One comms/datalink concern  
6. One thing you still need to ask an SME  

Optional: submit as a short `.md` for instructor review (same quality bar as A5 platform lines).

## Integrity note

Public product pages and museums/history are fine sources. Do **not** hunt classified material. If you do not know, write the **question**, not a guess dressed as fact.

## Self-check (5 min)

1. What is the **integrity rule** if you do not know an employment detail?  
2. Name three **platform categories** from the table (not three aircraft types).  
3. Why might **space / C2** systems still matter on an air CAP mission card?  
4. What six bullets must appear in the one-page platform brief?  
5. True/False: HIMARS is a great primary for a classic fighter CAP package.  

### Answers

1. Write the **SME question** — do not invent a fact.  
2. e.g. 5th-gen fighter, airlift, munitions/strike, missile defence, space/C2, rotary, multirole fighter, …  
3. Timing, warning, and battle-management **enablers** affect every ATO.  
4. Why primary; support role; fuel/tanker; sensors/SA; comms/datalink; SME question.  
5. **False** — wrong domain for primary fighter CAP (may appear only in joint vignettes, not as the CAP shooter).
