# A5 — Mini Mission Card

**Weight:** 15% · **Due:** Week 7 Thursday

## Prompt

Build an **unclassified** mission card for one mission type:

- Training / continuation, **or**  
- Air defense / CAP-style, **or**  
- Strike **as exercise** (no real targeting data)

Use the template from **ops-01-ato-planning**. Add the execution annex from **ops-02-ato-execution**. Justify platforms with public knowledge (**ops-03** / Tactical Library).

**Mission-type visuals:** see the illustrated cards in *ATO Planning* (`/static/images/mission-types/`) for CAP, strike, training, ISR/tanker, lift, and CSAR — pick one type and plan to that picture.

## Deliverables

1. Completed **mission card** (planning section — all template fields)  
2. **Execution annex:** 3 monitors, 2 change scenarios + responses, 1 observable success metric  
3. **Two platform choices** (public knowledge) with one sentence each why (primary + support)  
4. Optional: short AAR table if you ran a tabletop inject in class  

## Quality bar

- **Unclassified only** — no invented classified tactics or real target coordinates.  
- Planning factors (objective, assets, fuel, timing, comms, C2, contingencies) are visible on the card.  
- Execution annex is distinct from planning (what you monitor and how you flex).  
- Platforms fit the mission type; unknowns are phrased as SME questions, not fake facts.  
- A peer can **brief** the card in ~2 minutes.  

## Rubric

| Dimension | Max | What we look for |
|-----------|-----|------------------|
| operational_realism | 10 | Timing, fuel, comms, aborts, and C2 make sense at intern level |
| structure | 10 | Planning vs execution sections distinct; fields complete |
| communication | 5 | Clear enough for a peer to brief from |

*operational_realism includes respecting safety-of-flight framing and voice+datalink contingency thinking.*
