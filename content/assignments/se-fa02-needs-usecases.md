# SE-FA02 — Needs and two use-case briefs

**Time box:** 2 hours. Stop at 2 hours and turn in what you have.
**Due:** Week 2 Tuesday morning, before the Networking lecture
**Module:** se-f02-needs-usecases
**Weight:** 8% of this track’s intern-selection score.

## Prompt

Stay on the **same daily app** from SE-FA01 (or switch once, and say so). Do not jump to PRSAS yet.

## Deliverables

1. **Vision restated** in one sentence (from FA01 or improved).
2. **Three needs** in `As … we need … so that …` grammar. Mark which vision they derive_from.
3. **Use-case index** (name, actor, goal) with at least three rows.
4. **Two full briefs**: main success scenario + at least one extension each.
5. **One rejected use case** labeled *design / not a use case* and why.
6. **Trace table:** need → use case (traces_to).

## Quality bar

No UI widgets in the needs. Extensions are real alternate paths, not ‘error happens.’ A peer could write a shall from your brief.
