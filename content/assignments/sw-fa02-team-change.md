# SW-FA02 — Ticket → MR checklist

**Time box:** 2 hours. Stop at 2 hours and turn in what you have.
**Due:** Week 2 Thursday morning, before the Admin lecture
**Module:** sw-f02-team-change
**Weight:** 8% of this track’s intern-selection score.

## Prompt

Show the workflow, not a novel about Git internals.

## Deliverables

1. **Fake ticket** with ID, title, acceptance in two bullets.
2. **Branch name** derived from that ID.
3. **MR/PR description** (why, how to test, out of scope).
4. **Review checklist** (8–10 boxes) that would catch a real defect in code *or* a markdown ICD.
5. **Program vs lab table:** ticket, git host, review button, CI.
6. **Rule you will follow** when you are the only intern in the room.

## Quality bar

Checklist is specific. Description would let a peer press merge without Slack.

## Rubric

| Dimension | Max | What we look for |
|-----------|-----|------------------|
| completeness | 10 | All required artifacts present |
| accuracy | 10 | Technically right at intern level |
| communication | 5 | A peer can grade it in five minutes |
