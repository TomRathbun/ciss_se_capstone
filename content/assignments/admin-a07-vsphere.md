# ADMIN-A07 — vSphere Vocabulary & VM Lifecycle Brief

**Weight:** 5% · **Due:** After admin-07-vsphere-vsan-vdi · **Module:** admin-07-vsphere-vsan-vdi

## Prompt

Write an intern-level **ops brief** for virtualization support (lab or documented environment).

## Deliverables

1. **Vocabulary card:** ESXi, vCenter, vSphere, datastore/vSAN, port group, snapshot, template — one plain sentence each.
2. **VM lifecycle:** power ops, snapshot (when yes/no), clone vs template — risks of snapshot sprawl.
3. **Ticket evidence list:** what you capture before escalating a “VM down / cannot connect” incident (≥ 8 items).
4. **Capacity awareness:** one paragraph on disk/network objects that commonly break labs.
5. Optional: screenshot of vSphere inventory (redact names if required).

## Quality bar

- Snapshot advice is conservative.
- Escalation package is complete enough for a senior admin.
- No fake hostnames presented as production truth.

## Rubric

| Dimension | Max | What we look for |
|-----------|-----|------------------|
| vocabulary | 10 | Accurate definitions |
| operational_judgment | 10 | Lifecycle risks + evidence list |
| communication | 5 | Brief is scannable |
