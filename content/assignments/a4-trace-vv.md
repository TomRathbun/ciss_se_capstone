# A4 — Traceability & V&V Plan

**Weight:** 10% · **Due:** Week 9 Thursday · **Module:** se-08 Verification, Validation & Traceability  
**Cadence:** Assigned Monday after the V&V lecture. Due Thursday. Bring a draft RTM (at least 4 rows) if you want coaching.

## Prompt

Prove you can connect **your A2 requirements** to design and proof. Verification answers “did we build it right?” Validation answers “did we build the right thing?”

## Deliverables

1. **RTM** covering all FRs from A2 (or a declared subset ≥ 8 rows)  
   Columns: Req ID | Summary | Parent UC | Design element | Verification method (I/A/D/T) | Status | Notes  
2. **Verification plan** — half page: what you would test first and why; name the cheapest sufficient method per critical FR  
3. **Validation plan** — half page: how stakeholders confirm the right problem was solved (walkthrough, ops dry-run, …)  
4. **Orphan check:** one paragraph — any FR with no design? any design with no FR?  
5. One paragraph on a **risk** if verification is skipped  

## Rubric

traceability · vv_clarity · communication
